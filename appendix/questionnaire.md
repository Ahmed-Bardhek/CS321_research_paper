# Politeness Validation Questionnaire

This questionnaire validates whether the eight prompt templates are perceived as intended.

## Instructions for Respondents

Read each prompt template and rate only the tone of the request. Do not judge the difficulty of the academic question.

## Rating Scale

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Very impolite |
| 2 | Somewhat impolite |
| 3 | Neutral |
| 4 | Polite |
| 5 | Very polite |
